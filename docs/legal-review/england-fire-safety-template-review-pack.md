# England Fire-Safety Requirements Library — Template Review Pack

> **NOT APPLICABLE since 30 September 2026.** HostSafe was repositioned as a
> private property organiser that gives no legal, safety or compliance
> guidance. A fire-safety "Requirements Library" would contradict that
> positioning and must not be built. This pack is kept for history only.

**DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** These five templates must **not** be shown
in the HostSafe product, seeded into any database table, or labelled as
applying to any user's property until reviewed and approved by a qualified
England fire-safety/legal professional. No `guidance_templates` table or
related feature exists in the app at the time of writing.

## ⚑ Broad wording flagged for confirmation

The following phrases appear in the drafts below and need explicit
reviewer confirmation that they are not overstated:
- **"any self-catering holiday let"** (Template 1)
- **"every host"** (Template 2)
- **"essentially all"** (Template 3)
- **"every self-catering booking"** (Template 5)

## Template 1 — Written fire risk assessment

- **Proposed wording:** "As the person responsible for fire safety at your
  property, you're required to assess fire risks and write the assessment
  down in full — not just keep it in your head."
- **Proposed label:** Legal duty
- **Source URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/article/9
- **Source title:** The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005,
  Article 9
- **Source organisation:** legislation.gov.uk
- **Date checked:** 28 September 2026
- **Applicability wording:** "Any self-catering holiday let where anyone
  stays who isn't living there as their permanent home." ⚑ broad — see
  above.
- **Exceptions:** "Larger, more complex, shared, or converted properties
  should use a fuller assessment or a competent fire-risk assessor."
- **Disclaimer wording:** Standard Requirements Library disclaimer (see
  `current-disclaimer-audit.md` for house style).
- **Question requiring professional confirmation:** Is "Legal duty" the
  correct label for every self-catering property regardless of size, or
  are there scale/threshold exemptions under the Order or related
  regulations that should be reflected in the applicability wording?

## Template 2 — Review your fire risk assessment

- **Proposed wording:** "Fire risk assessments must be kept up to date —
  review yours regularly and whenever something about the property
  changes."
- **Proposed label:** Legal duty
- **Source URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/article/9
- **Source title:** The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005,
  Article 9(3)
- **Source organisation:** legislation.gov.uk
- **Date checked:** 28 September 2026
- **Applicability wording:** "Every host with a fire risk assessment." ⚑
  broad — see above.
- **Exceptions:** None identified.
- **Disclaimer wording:** Standard Requirements Library disclaimer.
- **Question requiring professional confirmation:** Is treating "review"
  as a standing legal duty (rather than folding it into Template 1 as a
  single item) the right framing, and is there any authoritative guidance
  on what "regularly" should mean that's missing here?

## Template 3 — Keep escape routes and exits clear

- **Proposed wording:** "Routes to your property's emergency exits, and
  the exits themselves, must be kept clear at all times, and exit doors
  must be openable without a key from the inside in an emergency."
- **Proposed label:** Legal duty
- **Source URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/article/14
- **Source title:** The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005,
  Article 14
- **Source organisation:** legislation.gov.uk
- **Date checked:** 28 September 2026
- **Direct quotes used:** "routes to emergency exits from premises and the
  exits themselves are kept clear at all times"; doors must not be
  "locked or fastened that they cannot be easily and immediately opened."
- **Applicability wording:** "Every property with a designated escape
  route (i.e. essentially all)." ⚑ broad — see above.
- **Exceptions:** "Properties with unusual layouts, shared escape routes
  with neighbours, or sliding/revolving doors as an exit need individual,
  professional attention."
- **Disclaimer wording:** Standard Requirements Library disclaimer.
- **Question requiring professional confirmation:** Does this general duty
  need any property-specific caveat beyond what's listed, particularly
  around shared/converted buildings?

## Template 4 — Smoke/heat alarm and detection checks

- **Proposed wording:** "Check and keep a record of testing your smoke and
  heat alarms, so you have evidence they're being looked after."
- **Proposed label:** Official guidance (not law)
- **Source URL:**
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/making-your-small-paying-guest-accommodation-safe-from-fire
- **Source title:** "Making your small paying guest accommodation safe
  from fire"
- **Source organisation:** Home Office / GOV.UK (first published 30 March
  2023, updated 20 January 2025); corroborated by Suffolk Fire and Rescue
  Service's published summary of the same guide
  (https://www.suffolk.gov.uk/suffolk-fire-and-rescue-service/fire-protection/fire-safety-for-self-catering-holiday-lets)
- **Date checked:** 28 September 2026
- **Applicability wording:** "Small self-catering properties matching the
  simplified guide's intended scope (simple layout, limited fire risks)."
- **Exceptions:** "The specific alarm grade/type needed depends on the
  property's layout — a competent assessor or the full guide should
  confirm what's right for a given property."
- **Disclaimer wording:** Standard Requirements Library disclaimer.
- **Question requiring professional confirmation:** The primary Home
  Office PDF could not be directly parsed by the tools available when this
  was researched — only a fire-and-rescue-service's paraphrase of it was
  read. **This wording must be checked against the primary Home Office PDF
  directly** before it's ever shown to users.

## Template 5 — Guest fire safety information

- **Proposed wording:** "Give guests clear fire safety information and an
  emergency plan when they arrive, so they know what to do and how to get
  out."
- **Proposed label:** Official guidance (not law)
- **Source URL:** Same as Template 4
- **Source title:** "Making your small paying guest accommodation safe
  from fire"
- **Source organisation:** Home Office / GOV.UK; corroborated by Suffolk
  Fire and Rescue Service: "provide fire safety information and an
  emergency plan to your guests."
- **Date checked:** 28 September 2026
- **Applicability wording:** "Every self-catering booking." ⚑ broad — see
  above.
- **Exceptions:** "Content should be tailored (e.g. accessibility needs of
  specific guests)."
- **Disclaimer wording:** Standard Requirements Library disclaimer.
- **Question requiring professional confirmation:** Same primary-source
  caveat as Template 4 — verify against the original PDF, not just the
  fire-and-rescue-service paraphrase.

## What is explicitly not included

No "Submit/send" template is proposed. No genuine, universally-applicable
"must be sent to a named organisation" requirement for small self-catering
holiday lets in England was identified during research — local
licensing/registration schemes vary by local authority and property
circumstances, and a generic one would risk being wrong for most users.

## Publication rule

These templates must not be shown in the product, seeded into the
database, or labelled as applying to users until reviewed by a qualified
England fire-safety/legal professional.
