## 2. The information we hold, and why

| What | Why we use it | Legal reason (UK GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| **Account details:** your email address and password (stored scrambled, so no one can read it) | To create your account and let you sign in | Needed for our contract with you |
| **Your property records:** property names and addresses, maintenance issues, actions and reminders, documents and renewal dates, calendar entries, notes | To run Letnook for you | Needed for our contract with you |
| **Files you upload:** documents, photos and property images, with their file names, sizes and upload dates | To store them privately and show them back to you | Needed for our contract with you |
| **Membership details:** your plan, billing period, trial and renewal dates, status, whether you hold a founding price, and reference codes from Stripe | To run your membership and decide what you can access | Needed for our contract with you |
| **Payment records:** what you paid and when (held by Stripe; see section 4) | To take payments and keep tax records | Contract, and our legal duty to keep financial records |
| **Sign-in cookies** | To keep you signed in securely | Needed for our contract with you |
| **Basic technical information** such as IP address and browser type, kept briefly by our hosting provider | To keep Letnook secure and working | Our legitimate interest in running a secure service |

We **don't** hold your card details. We don't use your information for
advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

**Information about other people.** If you record information about other
people, such as a guest's first name, a guest count or a booking reference,
you decide what to record. We store it on your behalf only to run Letnook for
you (see section 10 of our [Terms](/terms)). Letnook asks only for a guest's
first name, never surnames, contact details or payment information, and you
can remove a guest's name at any time. Cancelling a stay removes the name
automatically.

## 3. Emails

We only send emails needed to run your account: confirming your email
address, resetting your password, confirming email changes, and membership
emails such as trial reminders, receipts and failed-payment notices. We don't
send marketing emails. ⚑ Decision: if we ever want to, we'll ask first.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted companies to run Letnook. They can only use
your information to provide their service to us.

| Company | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Our database, file storage and sign-in system | [[⚑ Check: your project's region in Supabase → Project Settings, e.g. London (UK) or Ireland (EU)]] |
| **Vercel** | Hosts the Letnook website | Worldwide network, including the USA |
| **Stripe** | Takes card payments and runs memberships | UK, EU and the USA |

**Stripe** collects your card details directly, in a secure form provided by
Stripe. Your card details never reach Letnook. For card payments, Stripe also
acts independently to meet its own legal duties, such as preventing fraud, and
its own [privacy policy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy) explains how.

We'll only share your information with anyone else if the law requires it, or
to protect people's safety or our legal rights.

**Outside the UK.** Some of these companies may process information outside
the UK, including in the USA. When they do, they use protections approved
under UK law, such as the UK–US "data bridge" or the UK's international data
transfer agreement. ⚑ Check: confirm each provider's current safeguard in its
data processing terms.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Your account and records:** for as long as you have an account. If your
  membership ends, your records stay so you can still view, download or
  delete them (see our Terms). ⚑ Decision: we don't currently delete
  inactive accounts automatically.
- **When you delete something:** it's deleted from Letnook straight away.
- **When you delete your account:** your account, every record and every
  uploaded file are deleted straight away.
- **Backups:** our database provider keeps short-term backups for disaster
  recovery, which are replaced automatically. Deleted information can remain
  in these for up to [[7]] days before it's gone for good. [[⚑ Check: matches
  your Supabase plan's backup retention.]]
- **Payment records:** Stripe and we keep records of payments for as long as
  tax law requires, normally up to 6 years, even after an account is deleted.
- **Technical logs:** kept by our hosting provider for a short period only.

## 6. Keeping it secure

- Your password is stored scrambled (hashed) and never visible to us.
- Every account's records are kept separate by rules inside the database
  itself, so one member can't see another's information.
- Uploaded files are stored privately and are only shown to the account that
  uploaded them.
- Connections to Letnook are encrypted.
- Card details are handled only by Stripe.
- Our error logs record error codes only, not the content of your records.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **see** the information we hold about you (a "subject access request");
- **correct** anything that's wrong (most things you can edit yourself);
- **delete** your information (you can delete records or your whole account
  yourself at any time);
- **get a copy** of information you gave us, in a usable format;
- **object to** or ask us to **limit** how we use your information in some
  cases.

To use any of these rights, email [[contact email]]. We'll reply within one
month. It's free.

## 8. Cookies

Letnook only uses cookies that are **strictly necessary**: to keep you signed
in and secure, and (on the payment page) Stripe's cookies for secure payments
and fraud prevention. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, so we
don't need to ask for cookie consent. If that ever changes, we'll ask first.

## 9. Age

Letnook is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect
information about children.

## 10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us
first at [[contact email]]. You also have the right to complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or
0303 123 1113.

## 11. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice if how we use information changes, and we'll email
you about important changes.
