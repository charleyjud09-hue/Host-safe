## 9. Liability — ⚑ FLAGGED FOR SOLICITOR REVIEW
**Do not draft a blanket liability exclusion.** A clause claiming to
remove all liability or legal responsibility is very likely unenforceable
against a consumer under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 and/or the
Consumer Rights Act 2015, and could itself create legal risk if published
without proper drafting. This section must be written by a solicitor, not
generated automatically.
