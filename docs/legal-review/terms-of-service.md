## 2. What Letnook is, and what it isn't

Letnook is a **private organiser** for holiday lets and short-term rentals. It
helps you keep your own maintenance records, reminders, documents, renewal
dates and guest stays in one place.

Letnook **does not**:

- give legal, safety, fire-safety, tax or compliance advice;
- check, inspect, certify or approve any property;
- tell you which laws, rules or deadlines apply to you;
- send anything to any authority, insurer or booking platform for you;
- act as an official records store.

**You are responsible** for your property, for meeting your legal obligations,
for deciding which requirements apply to you, and for getting professional
advice where you need it. Reminders come only from dates you enter, so they
won't cover every requirement or deadline that applies to you.

Letnook isn't affiliated with Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo or any other platform.

## 3. Who can use Letnook

Each account is for one person. Keep your password private. Tell us straight

## 5. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in **Account settings → Subscription & billing**.
You don't need to contact us.

- **During the free trial:** you won't be charged anything.
- **After the trial:** you won't be charged again. You keep access until the
  end of the period you've already paid for. We don't give partial refunds
  for the rest of that period, except as set out in section 6.

## 6. Your 14-day right to cancel

By law you have **14 days to cancel** from the day your membership starts
(the day you start your free trial, or the day you join again if you're a
returning member), without giving a reason.

- If you started with the 30-day free trial, no payment is taken in those 14
  days, so cancelling simply means you pay nothing.
- If you joined again and paid straight away, you asked for your membership to
  start immediately. If you cancel within 14 days, we'll refund you, minus a
  fair amount for the days you had access.

To cancel, use Account settings or email us at [[contact email]].

## 7. If a payment fails

If a payment can't be taken, Stripe will try again over the following days and
we'll let you know so you can update your card. Your access continues while
this happens. If payment still can't be taken, your membership ends and your
account becomes read-only (see section 8). A founding member price ends at the
same time.

## 8. When a membership ends: your records stay yours

If your membership or trial ends, your account becomes **read-only**. You can
still sign in, view, download and delete everything, but you can't add or
change records until you join again.

We keep your records until you delete them or delete your account. ⚑ Decision:
we don't currently delete inactive accounts automatically. If we ever
introduce a policy for long-inactive accounts, we'll email you well in advance.
